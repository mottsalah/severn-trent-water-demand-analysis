# Project Scope

## Severn Trent Water Demand Forecasting and Scenario Analysis Model

### Objective
Build an interview-ready public-data prototype that demonstrates how published water demand forecasts can be cleaned, reconciled and stress-tested under alternative demand-management and growth assumptions.

### Primary source
Severn Trent published WRMP24 market information workbook for the Strategic Grid Water Resource Zone:
`Water-resources-market-information22-Strategic-Grid.xlsx`

The source contains published baseline demand, supply and supply-demand balance trajectories, including household and non-household consumption, PCC, leakage, population, metering, WAFU and target headroom.

### Core analytical questions
1. How does published Strategic Grid demand change across the planning horizon?
2. Which demand components drive the change?
3. How does the baseline supply-demand balance evolve?
4. How sensitive is demand to PCC, population, non-household consumption and leakage assumptions?
5. How much can illustrative demand-management scenarios improve the supply-demand balance?

### Important boundary
This is an independent public-data prototype. It is not Severn Trent's internal forecasting model and does not reproduce proprietary systems such as TARGET, Netbase, CACI InSite or Aquator.
