# KPI Definitions

- **Distribution Input (Ml/d):** water entering the distribution system to meet demand.
- **Household Consumption (Ml/d):** metered plus unmetered household consumption.
- **Non-Household Consumption (Ml/d):** metered plus unmetered non-household consumption.
- **PCC (l/h/d):** average household per-capita consumption in litres per head per day.
- **Leakage (Ml/d):** published total leakage volume per day.
- **WAFU (Ml/d):** total Water Available For Use, including transfers.
- **Target Headroom (Ml/d):** planning allowance for uncertainty.
- **Supply-Demand Balance (Ml/d):** total WAFU minus distribution input minus target headroom.
- **Demand Change vs Baseline (Ml/d):** scenario distribution input minus published baseline distribution input.
